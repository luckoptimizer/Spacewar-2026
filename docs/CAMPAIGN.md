# Operation HIGH GROUND

Classification for play: UNCLASSIFIED // FICTION

## Frame

December 2019 created a sixth armed service so the United States could organize, train, and equip forces for the orbital domain. This campaign treats that fact as the opening chord, then jumps to a 2026 contested-space exercise: five theaters, one Gold Eagle hull, one opposing wedge that learns.

This is fiction. It is not doctrine.

## Acts

### SF-01 — LEO / GOLD DOME
Low Earth orbit. Prove the 1962 duel still decides a fight when the well is Earth and the other pilot is a machine.

Win: destroy the opposing wedge.

### SF-02 — LUNAR LAGRANGE
Quiet gravity. The opponent no longer comes alone. Sentinel drones detach and hunt.

Win: destroy the wedge. Drones are optional kills.

### SF-03 — MARS APPROACH
A heavier well and a directed-energy battery. Heat-manage the AEGIS beam or it dies when you need it.

Win: destroy the wedge without kissing the well.

### SF-04 — BELT AMBUSH
Rocks do not take sides. They take hull.

Win: destroy the wedge. Asteroids are terrain and ammunition if you sling them.

### SF-05 — JOVIAN GATE
The well is cruel. The AI calls drones and leads every shot. Finish the exercise.

Win: last hull standing.

## House line (flavor, not policy)

“Space is the high ground. The high ground is not a speech. It is a ship that still has fuel.”
